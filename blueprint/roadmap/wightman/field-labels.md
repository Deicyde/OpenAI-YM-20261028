# Field labels

A **space of field labels** is a complex vector space $L$: a set $L$, an
addition making $L$ an abelian group, and a scalar multiplication by complex
numbers making $L$ a vector space over $\mathbb C$. The set, the addition and
the scalar multiplication are data; the conditions are the axioms of an abelian
group and of a complex vector space. The elements of $L$ are called labels.

No topology, norm or inner product on $L$ is part of this structure, and the
dimension of $L$ is not restricted.
