# Energy–momentum measure

Let $H$ be a Hilbert space with norm $\lVert\cdot\rVert$ and inner product
$\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$, conjugate-linear in its first argument and linear
in its second. Let $\mathbb R^4$ be spacetime, with its Euclidean topology, and
call a subset of $\mathbb R^4$ Borel when it belongs to the $\sigma$-algebra
generated by the open sets. A bounded operator on $H$ is a continuous
complex-linear map $H \to H$; $0$ and $1$ denote the zero and the identity
operator, $ST$ the composite of bounded operators $S$ and $T$, with $T$ applied
first, and $T^*$ the adjoint of a bounded operator $T$, the bounded operator
with $\langle T^*\psi, \chi \rangle = \langle \psi, T\chi \rangle$ for all
$\psi, \chi \in H$.

An **energy–momentum measure** on $H$ consists of two pieces of data:

- for every subset $A \subseteq \mathbb R^4$, Borel or not, a bounded operator
  $E(A)$ on $H$;
- for every vector $\psi \in H$, a measure $\mu_\psi$ on the Borel sets of
  $\mathbb R^4$, with values in $[0, \infty]$, called the diagonal measure of
  $\psi$.

These data are subject to seven conditions:

1. $E(\varnothing) = 0$.
2. $E(\mathbb R^4) = 1$.
3. $E(A)\,E(B) = E(A \cap B)$ for all Borel sets $A, B \subseteq \mathbb R^4$.
4. $E(A)^* = E(A)$ for every Borel set $A \subseteq \mathbb R^4$.
5. For every sequence $A_0, A_1, A_2, \dots$ of pairwise disjoint Borel subsets
   of $\mathbb R^4$, that is, with $A_i \cap A_j = \varnothing$ whenever
   $i \neq j$, and for every vector $\psi \in H$, the series
   $\sum_i E(A_i)\,\psi$ converges unconditionally in $H$ to
   $E\big(\bigcup_i A_i\big)\,\psi$: for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a
   finite set $F_0$ of indices such that
   $\big\lVert \sum_{i \in F} E(A_i)\,\psi - E\big(\bigcup_i A_i\big)\,\psi \big\rVert < \varepsilon$
   for every finite set $F$ of indices containing $F_0$.
6. Every diagonal measure is finite: $\mu_\psi(\mathbb R^4) < \infty$ for every
   $\psi \in H$.
7. For every Borel set $A \subseteq \mathbb R^4$ and every vector $\psi \in H$,
   $\mu_\psi(A) = \langle \psi, E(A)\,\psi \rangle$, where the value
   $\mu_\psi(A) \in [0, \infty]$ is converted to a real number, a finite value
   to itself and $\infty$ to $0$, and that real number is read as a complex
   number.

The operators $E(A)$ are given for every subset of $\mathbb R^4$, but conditions
3, 4, 5 and 7 concern Borel sets only, and conditions 1 and 2 the sets
$\varnothing$ and $\mathbb R^4$. The diagonal measures are data of their own,
related to the operators by condition 7 alone. The sets and measures live on
the same $\mathbb R^4$ as spacetime, with the same coordinates; nothing in this
definition distinguishes one coordinate from another.
