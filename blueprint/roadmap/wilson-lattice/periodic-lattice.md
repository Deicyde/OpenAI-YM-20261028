# Periodic lattice

For a positive integer $M$ ($M = 1$ included), the **periodic lattice of side
$M$** is the set

$$\Lambda_M = (\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z)^4$$

of maps that assign to each of the four axes $0, 1, 2, 3$ a residue class
modulo $M$. Its elements are the **sites**, and $x_i \in \mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z$
denotes the coordinate of the site $x$ on axis $i$. The axes are the
coordinate axes of spacetime, numbered from $0$, and axis $3$ is the time axis.
Sites are added coordinatewise modulo $M$.
