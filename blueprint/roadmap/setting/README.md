# Setting

The construction takes place on finite periodic lattices of four-dimensional
Euclidean space, with a compact matrix gauge group acting on the links. This
part fixes that setting: the lattice, its paths and its subdivisions, the
symmetries of space and of Minkowski spacetime, the gauge groups with their
Lie algebras and Haar measure, and the Wilson measure on lattice gauge
configurations. Armstrong's informalization treats it as a single unit (I.1).
