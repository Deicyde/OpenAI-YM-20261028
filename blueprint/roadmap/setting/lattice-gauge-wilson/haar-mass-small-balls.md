# Haar mass of small chord balls

Let $(n, G)$ be a gauge group, with its Borel sets and its Haar probability
measure $h_G$, and let $\chi$ be its normalized chord. Let $D$ be the
dimension of $M_{n+1}(\mathbb C)$ as a real vector space, where real scalars
multiply each entry; thus $D = 2(n+1)^2$.

**Theorem.** For every real number $r$ with $0 < r \le 1$,

$$\left(\frac{r}{4}\right)^{D} \le h_G\big(\{u \in G : \chi(u) \le r\}\big).$$

The right side is the $h_G$-measure of the closed chord ball of radius $r$,
taken as a real number. The exponent $D$ is the real dimension of the
ambient matrix space $M_{n+1}(\mathbb C)$, not the dimension of $G$ or of its
Lie algebra. The theorem assumes nothing about $G$ beyond its being a gauge
group; in particular it assumes no simplicity.
