# Phases and reconstruction

The infinite-volume limit of the construction need not be a single pure
theory: its Schwinger functions are an average over a space of phases. This
part decomposes the limit into its phases, reconstructs a Wightman theory from
Euclidean data by the Osterwalder–Schrader procedure, and carries the
reconstruction out in every phase at once, with uniform spectral bounds and
the stress tensor. Armstrong's informalization divides it into three units:
the decomposition of the limit into phases (V.1), Osterwalder–Schrader
reconstruction of a Wightman theory (V.2), and reconstruction for every phase
and the reconstructed theory (V.3). This version of the book maps the last.
