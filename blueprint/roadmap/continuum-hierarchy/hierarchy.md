# Continuum hierarchy

Let $C$ be a Wilson family for a matrix gauge group. Write
$\mathbb N = \{0, 1, 2, \dots\}$. Spacetime is $\mathbb R^4$ and space is
$\mathbb R^3$, each with its Euclidean norm, and for $n \in \mathbb N$ the
configurations of $n$ points form the space $(\mathbb R^4)^n$ of $n$-tuples
$x = (x_0, \dots, x_{n-1})$ of points of spacetime, with the Euclidean norm of
$\mathbb R^{4n}$.

A **continuum hierarchy** over $C$ consists of the nineteen components listed
below. Components 5, 10 and 11 are conditions; all the others are data. Once
the spaces $F_d$ of component 4 are given, a **graded field** is a pair
$(d, u)$ of a natural number $d$, its *grade*, and a vector $u \in F_d$. The
set of graded fields is the disjoint union

$$
\mathcal F = \bigsqcup_{d \in \mathbb N} F_d
= \{ (d, u) : d \in \mathbb N,\ u \in F_d \},
$$

so two graded fields are equal only when they have the same grade and the
same vector.

1. A Hilbert space $H$ (data).
2. A space of field labels $L$ (data).
3. A Wightman theory on $H$ and $L$ (data).
4. For every $d \in \mathbb N$, a complex normed vector space $F_d$, the
   *field space of grade $d$* (data): a set with an addition, a
   multiplication by complex numbers and a norm, satisfying the axioms of a
   complex vector space and of a norm.
5. For every $d \in \mathbb N$, the space $F_d$ is finite-dimensional over
   $\mathbb C$ (condition).
6. For every $d \in \mathbb N$, a complex-linear map
   $\iota_d : F_d \to L$ (data).
7. A map $\pi$ from the set of tokens of $C$ to $\mathcal F$, which assigns a
   graded field $\pi(v)$ to every token $v$ (data).
8. A set $X$, whose elements are called *characters*, with a metric on $X$
   (data).
9. A σ-algebra on $X$ (data).
10. This σ-algebra is the Borel σ-algebra of the metric topology of $X$, the
    σ-algebra generated by its open sets (condition).
11. $X$ is compact in its metric topology (condition).
12. A measure $\nu$ on this σ-algebra, with values in $[0, \infty]$, the
    *phase measure* (data).
13. A character $s_0 \in X$, the *selected character* (data).
14. The *correlations* (data): for every $n \in \mathbb N$, every
    $s \in X \cup \{*\}$, where $*$ is one additional value that is not a
    character, every $n$-tuple $a = (a_0, \dots, a_{n-1}) \in \mathcal F^n$
    of graded fields and every configuration $x \in (\mathbb R^4)^n$ of $n$
    points, a complex number $S_s(a; x)$. The correlations are defined at
    every configuration, those with coincident points included. The
    correlations $S_*(a; x)$ are called *unselected*.
15. The *continuation* (data): for every $n \in \mathbb N$, every $n$-tuple
    $a \in \mathcal F^n$ of graded fields, every $n$-tuple
    $z = (z_0, \dots, z_{n-1}) \in \mathbb C^n$ of complex numbers and every
    $n$-tuple $\xi = (\xi_0, \dots, \xi_{n-1}) \in (\mathbb R^3)^n$ of points
    of space, a complex number $S^c(a; z, \xi)$. The continuation has no
    argument from $X \cup \{*\}$.
16. For every $j \in \mathbb N$, a finite set $\mathrm{Ch}_j$, whose elements
    are called *channels* (data).
17. For every $j \in \mathbb N$, a map $A_j : \mathrm{Ch}_j \to F_j$, the
    *actor* (data).
18. The *coefficients* (data): for every finite set $I$, every family
    $a = (a_i)_{i \in I}$ of graded fields, every $j \in \mathbb N$, every
    channel $\kappa \in \mathrm{Ch}_j$, every family $x = (x_i)_{i \in I}$ of
    points of spacetime and every point $o$ of spacetime, a complex number
    $C^{j,\kappa}_a(x; o)$.
19. A vector $\Theta \in F_4$, the *centered trace* (data).

The family $C$ enters only through its set of tokens, the domain of $\pi$.
Apart from conditions 5, 10 and 11, the definition imposes no condition
beyond those built into the notions it uses: Hilbert space, space of field
labels, Wightman theory, normed vector space, linear map, metric, σ-algebra,
measure and finite set. In particular, the Wightman theory is not required
to satisfy the Wightman axioms, the phase measure is not required to be
finite, no continuity or other regularity of the correlations, the
continuation or the coefficients is required, and no condition relates the
Wightman theory, the correlations, the continuation, the channels, the
actors, the coefficients and the centered trace to one another.
